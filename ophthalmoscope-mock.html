WHAT CHANGED

A different tool, not a wider retinoscope.

  1. Optical headA broad rounded shell gives the direct scope its own silhouette.
  2. Viewing sideA soft eye cup and small rear aperture tell the learner where to look.
  3. Lens wheelA visible indexed dial suggests selectable lenses without copying a branded device.
  4. Front faceThe illumination window and protective brow separate the patient-facing side.
