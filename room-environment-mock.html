OPTO · ROOM ENVIRONMENT MOCK

Consultation 01: current vs proposed room

Same 4.15 × 5.15 × 3 m footprint, station positions and patient eye anchors as ConsultingRoomShell and Room.tsx. Only the room's surfaces, lighting and furnishing change. The patient is a neutral clay stand-in so you can judge the room by itself.

Surfaces

  • Dark slate wall behind the patient (#2f4448). Corneal reflexes, the red reflex and the chart all stand out against it, and the patient's face is no longer lost against pale green.
  • Warm off-white side walls (#ebe7df) with a faint paint texture, replacing four slightly different grey-greens.
  • Oak-effect clinical vinyl in planks with grain. It gives scale and depth that the 0.5 m grid lines didn't.
  • Suspended ceiling tile grid with recessed LED panels, plus proper skirting, door frame and window reveal.

Lighting

  • Replace flat ambient (0.55) + hemi (1.05) with two ceiling area lights + window daylight + one soft key over the chair. The room gets shadows and falloff.
  • Image-based environment light (RoomEnvironment PMREM) so chrome, plastic and the chart screen reflect something.
  • Exam dim is a real state: panels drop to about 8%, blinds close and the chart backlight remains. It matches “DIM ROOM LIGHTS” for pupils and retinoscopy.
  • Soft contact shadows under furniture. On Quest these should be baked decals, not real-time shadows.

Furnishing

  • Wall-mounted LCD acuity display with a bezel and backlight, replacing the dark box and “SIMULATED DISPLAY” sign. The chart stays at the existing acuity anchor.
  • Left-wall worktop: base units, sink, sanitiser, towel dispenser and upper cabinets. It reads as a clinic and fills the dead wall.
  • Ophthalmic chair with a hydraulic column, headrest and footrest. The examiner gets a stool. The trolley and refraction unit keep their positions but get chrome, brushed metal and rounded shells.
  • Small cues: wall clock, eye anatomy poster, door plaque, bin. These are cheap and add a lot of believability.

Shipping on Quest

  • Everything here is procedural, with no third-party assets, so docs/asset-manifest.md stays clean.
  • For production, bake lighting into lightmaps (Blender → glTF with KTX2 textures). Keep only 1 real-time shadow light, near the patient.
  • Merge static room meshes into a few draw calls and keep texture sizes at 1k or below.
  • Layout question: the chart is on the rear wall behind the patient, who faces the examiner. A real room would use a mirror or a reversed chart. That's a clinical/layout decision to review, not a visual one.
