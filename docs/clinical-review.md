### Cover-test sustained deviation update (2026-10-14) — draft, review required

The XR cover–uncover and alternating-cover lessons no longer show a fixed 4 mm/3 mm “pulse” that springs back to straight. Eye position is now a continuous function of where the occluder physically is (`web/interaction/coverDeviation.ts`):

- **Unilateral tropia** (left esotropia): OS rests turned in before any cover. Covering OD makes OS refixate (move out) and hold fixation while OD deviates behind the cover. Uncovering OD returns fixation to OD and OS turns back in. Covering OS produces no visible movement. Primary and secondary deviation are treated as equal (simplification).
- **Phoria** (exophoria): both eyes straight while fusing. The covered eye drifts out behind the occluder; on uncovering it moves back in by fusional recovery.
- **Alternating cover**: the covered eye takes the deviated position and the uncovered eye refixates. The occluder passing between the eyes holds the last state, so fusion is not regained. (Vertical laterality and binocular coupling were corrected in the update below.)
- **Prism**: superseded by the alternating-cover coupling update below. Partial prism reduces movement, the endpoint stops it, overcorrection reverses it, and a wrong base increases it.
- **Timing (illustrative)**: refixation ~45 s⁻¹ (about 50 ms to 90%), fusional recovery ~9 s⁻¹ (about 0.25 s), drift behind the cover ~3 s⁻¹ (about 0.75 s). These follow the qualitative textbook distinction (fast refixation, slower drift and recovery) and are not measured saccade or vergence dynamics.
- **Magnitude**: Δ is converted to an angle (atan(Δ/100)) and then to iris shift using an 11 mm iris-to-rotation-centre arm, relative to a 5.9 mm real iris radius. The clinic face is drawn larger than life, so the shift is scaled to the rendered iris, which keeps the same proportion of the iris as on a real eye (12Δ ≈ 24% of the iris radius). The motility lesson still uses its own separate, unscaled gaze offset.
- **New illustrative magnitudes (not from a clinical source):** left esotropia **20Δ**, exophoria **10Δ**. These affect only the visual size of the movement; the recorded answer for cover–uncover remains the timing pattern. The alternating-cover magnitudes are unchanged (exo 12Δ, eso 18Δ, vertical 8Δ and 10Δ; see the laterality update below).

Decisions for the reviewer: whether 20Δ/10Δ are suitable teaching magnitudes; whether primary and secondary deviation should differ; whether the timing constants convey refixation versus drift adequately; and The desktop trainer now uses the same model and magnitudes, scaled to its own drawn iris; it keeps prism before the eye from the first prism repeat until the sequence is reset. Status remains **draft**.

### Alternating-cover coupling and vertical laterality update (2026-10-14) — draft, review required

Errors found in the first sustained model and corrected (`web/interaction/coverDeviation.ts`, both XR and desktop):

- **Vertical deviations moved both eyes in the same direction.** The phoria is now modelled as a relation between the eyes. Horizontal `h` is the sum of both eyes' temporal rotation (exo +), so both eyes move alike. Vertical `v` is OD relative to OS (right hyper +), so on alternating cover the eyes move in **opposite** directions.
- **Covered eye now yoked to the refixation (Hering's law).** It previously drifted slowly on every shift, so fast alternation under-showed the deviation. Slow drift (~0.75 s to 90%) now applies only to the first dissociation from binocular viewing; every later shift shows the full deviation. The first shift after that drift may therefore appear smaller if the first cover is held briefly.
- **Eye looking through a prism turns toward its apex** (base in → temporal, base out → nasal, base up → down, base down → up, by the prism power). The covered eye keeps the phoria relation to the fixating eye. At the endpoint the eye behind the prism stays visibly turned and neither eye moves. Fusion is assumed to overcome any prism while both eyes are uncovered; fusional amplitude limits are not modelled.
- **Neutralisation is computed from the model**, with no movement in either eye within 0.5Δ. It replaces an exact authored-base match. Either prism side is accepted: horizontal base before either eye; for a right hypo, base up before OD **or** base down before OS. The wrong-eye vertical base is rejected.

**New draft clinical content (no clinical source; reviewer decision required):**
- The two vertical alternating-cover cases are assigned to the right eye: **right hypo 8Δ** (= left hyper) and **right hyper 10Δ** (= left hypo). Magnitudes are unchanged.
- Recording asks for **OD's movement on uncovering** and a deviation chosen from Exo / Eso / R hyper (L hypo) / R hypo (L hyper). Prism feedback names the eye the prism was before.

Desktop technique corrections: binocular exposure now restarts alternating cover as in XR (0.35 s). On desktop only lowering the occluder clear of the face counts as exposure; crossing the nose is neither timed nor credited, because keyboard speed is not hand speed. Choosing the movement no longer fills in the deviation. A required “Prism before OD/OS” choice positions the prism bar before that eye.

Decisions for the reviewer: right-eye assignment of the vertical cases; the recording format (OD-movement field and paired R/L labels); whether to show reduced first-shift movement; whether fusional limits with large prisms should be modelled. Status remains **draft**.
