# VR orientation and visible-geometry audit — 28 October 2026

Reviewed the current `vr-improvements` working tree, including the uncommitted pickup and report-material optimizations. This is a further audit, not a correction of the findings below. Application behavior, authored findings and clinical gates were not changed during this review.

Six diagnostic checks reproduced the following mismatches using mounted R3F geometry, actual world transforms, Three.js ray intersections and, for O5, the active NRA/PRA controller. These are software reproductions, not physical headset or clinician acceptance. All six are P2: they affect visible equipment, technique fidelity or guidance consistency; no Test scoring corruption was demonstrated.

## Confirmed findings

| ID | Finding and effect | Evidence and correction direction |

| O1 | **The accepted near-card pose presents its blank side to the patient.** The declared working direction disagrees with the modeled print face. This affects the common near card used by horizontal-near vergence, vergence facility, minus-lens, NRA/PRA and accommodative facility. | `ConsultationInstruments.tsx:58` pitches `NearCardModel` into a vertical plane. Its print originates on local −Y (`ExamInstrumentModels.tsx:147` onwards), becoming root −Z. The tool declares +Z forward (`xrConsultationTools.ts:64`), and the stand applies a 180° yaw (`xrPracticeBatch.ts:11`). The diagnostic confirms the print is on the side away from the patient while `libraryNear` accepts that pose. Reconcile model face, declared forward, grip rotation and stand rotation together; changing only the readiness check would strand existing placement. |
| O2 | **The added readable near-card text is perpendicular to the card.** “C D H N O / READ THIS LINE” lies in a horizontal plane instead of on the patient-facing card surface. The geometry check can therefore accept a “readable” target whose letters are edge-on. | `ConsultationInstruments.tsx:59` gives the separate `XRSign` a 90° X rotation and places it at root Y = −0.011, while the card is already vertical. The diagnostic measures a dot product of zero between its text-plane normal and the card-face normal. This sign is enabled by the minus-lens and NRA/PRA adapters. Put the text on the same defined patient-facing surface as the card; verify plane normal, offset, upright text and visibility after fitting. |

## Checks and limits

- Inspected shared grip/ray rotations, working points, fitting sockets, near-card and ruler targets, sensory faces, rod fitting, prism-base sampling, eye anchors and the patient/examiner booklet faces.
- Canonical OD is at negative world X and OS at positive world X for a learner facing the patient. Red/green filter assignment follows those anchors; no general OD/OS reversal was found in the inspected code.
- The dedicated 4Δ prism has a separately modeled base axis and a patient-temporal direction check. Its existing mounted regressions passed. Maddox’s O6 should not be generalized to that tool.
- Maddox’s rod grooves and fitting sockets physically rotate together. The visible rod rotation is separate from the prism-bar base discrepancy.
- The scope model’s authored aperture centre and shared working point were cross-checked in source; no additional offset defect was established. Physical grip ergonomics, stereo comfort, actual device culling and headset readability remain unmeasured.
- Focused verification: **6 files / 79 tests passed**, including the six new diagnostic probes plus existing consultation-tool, sensory-equipment, Maddox-engine, 4Δ and stereo mounted tests. The full suite/build were not rerun for this documentation-only audit. E2E remains excluded at the user’s request.

The probes assert the current faulty behavior to establish evidence; they are not acceptance tests for a fix.
